# Code of Conduct

Contributors are expected to act professionally and respectfully in issues, code review and project discussions.

We welcome corrections supported by evidence, constructive disagreement and technical criticism focused on the work. Harassment, threats, personal attacks, discriminatory language and deliberate disclosure of private information are unacceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content, ask participants to disengage or restrict participation when necessary. Enforcement decisions should consider the context and severity of the behavior.

Report conduct concerns privately to **joshuakusaasira@gmail.com** with relevant context. Do not include personal details in a public issue. No guaranteed response deadline is represented by this policy.
